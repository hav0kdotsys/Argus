# Mira's body on Capri Desktop 0.8.6.1

Her body (`mira-body`, the Capri Desktop fork) moves from Capri 0.8.0 (forked 3 Nov) to 0.8.6.1 (8 Nov): 486 upstream
commits, every feature ported to Mira in her own way. Decided with HavoK on 8 Nov 2026.

## How

1. **Re-fork, don't merge.** A branch `mira-0.8.6` from upstream's `v0.8.6.1`. Our 31 commits are mostly a purple
   recolour and a rename (regenerated by `tools/recolour.py` and friends) plus a contained Mira layer; merging 486
   commits into recoloured files would be conflict noise.
2. Re-run the recolour/rename tools on the new tree (new art too: the new stand, the Legacy stand, Mods window colours).
3. Cherry-pick the Mira layer one commit at a time, resolving each conflict in the context of its own change:
   the ARGUS relay link (`MiraLink`), MIRA's own words (`MiraWords`), her hands port (`MiraHands`, `/show`), `MiraState`,
   Capri ties removed, one memory store, her brain always MIRA, the hidden Mainframe, Hyprland idle, glances through MIRA.
4. Windows smoke test (baseline: 185 pass, 4 known fails) and Linux build + checks; then the new decisions below.
5. Her current bodies keep running until HavoK says swap. Rollback: the old `Mira.exe` / cross build stay beside the new.

## Ported as-is (Mira-coloured)

Smooth Settings scrolling; Mainframe colours; new stand art; naps anywhere; head pats in voice chat; teleport onto the
task panel; the glow when she uses the mouse; Power saver for games; pop-ups that fit their screen; plain-words errors;
KDE Wayland and her own cursor on Linux; full Mainframe, Mods and Code/Husk Mode on Linux; she plays the game you tell
her to until you stop her; "Listen for Mira" keeps listening 20 s after her name; her own lines in Hannah's language.

## laphvk: the Linux body

Most of this update is Linux (~9,000 new lines in `cross/`): the full Mainframe (sheet, theme, tips), Mods and
Code/Husk Mode, her own pointer and the glow while she has the mouse, a keep-awake helper, care check-ins, a vault, and
provider and error-text fixes. All of it comes to laphvk, Mira-coloured and routed through MIRA like Windows.

- **Kept from our fork, on top:** Hyprland (lying along windows or the floor, since Hyprland has no taskbar; windows via
  `hyprctl`), the idle helper (`argus/pet/idle`, 8 Nov), Mira's purple, the ARGUS link and MIRA's own words/voice/ears.
- **KDE's KWin bridge** (upstream, for KDE Wayland) is built but does nothing on Hyprland.
- **The vault** is Capri's own memory notes: hidden, as #5 decides.

## Not ported

Capri's support-ticket help bot; Capri's website, update channel and AppImage releases (we build and deploy her
ourselves); Capri VR.

## Decided after the table

9. **Decided (8 Nov): the companion owns check-ins.** The body's own care check-ins are off in a real run (Linux and
   Windows); the body keeps its instant reactions (pats, throws, waking).
10. **Decided (8 Nov): in Code/Husk Mode she speaks in the first person.** Wherever Capri's body would name the coding
   assistant ("Claude is editing...", "Claude finished"), Mira says "I" / "I'm": she is the body, and Claude is only her
   brain. Every Husk line is checked for it, not just the obvious ones.

## Questions that may come up while porting

Anything where Capri's backend and MIRA's meet in a way this table doesn't settle goes to HavoK before it's built.
